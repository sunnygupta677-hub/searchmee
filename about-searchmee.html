SearchMee - India's Upcoming Local Search Platform

SearchMee is an upcoming local search platform designed to help users discover nearby shops, services, restaurants and businesses across India.

The platform is currently in pre-launch phase and starting with cities like Delhi, Noida, Ghaziabad, Greater Noida, Lucknow and Agra.

What Users Can Find on SearchMee

  • AC Repair Services
  • Plumbers
  • Electricians
  • Restaurants
  • Salons
  • Gyms
  • Medical Stores

SearchMee for Shop Owners

Shop owners and local businesses can register their business on SearchMee to increase visibility and reach more nearby customers.
