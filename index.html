Privacy Policy

SearchMee · Last Updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SearchMee (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you use the SearchMee mobile app and the website searchmee.in. SearchMee is a local business discovery platform that helps people find nearby shops and services in India.

1. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect your mobile number for OTP verification, along with basic profile details you choose to provide.

B. Location Information

With your permission, SearchMee may access your device’s precise or approximate location to identify your current location or area, show nearby shops and businesses, provide location-based search results, calculate approximate distance between you and listed businesses, and improve the relevance of local search results.

Location access is used for location-based features and is not intended for continuous background tracking. You can control or disable location permission through your device settings. If location permission is disabled, some nearby-location features may not work correctly.

C. Business Information

If you register a shop or business on SearchMee, we may collect information such as:

  • Business/shop name
  • Business category
  • Address/location
  • Contact number
  • Business hours
  • Business description
  • Business photos/images, where provided
  • Other information submitted by the business owner

D. Device and Usage Information

We may collect limited technical and usage information, such as:

  • Device type/model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Crash/error information
  • App usage and interaction information
  • Diagnostic information

This information may be used to maintain, secure, and improve SearchMee.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify users through OTP/authentication
  • Provide nearby shop and business search
  • Display relevant local businesses
  • Allow users to contact businesses
  • Provide location-based features
  • Allow businesses to create and manage listings
  • Process applicable payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect fraud, misuse, or security issues
  • Fix technical problems and improve the app
  • Send important service-related notifications
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. Location-Based Search

SearchMee is designed to provide local shop discovery. When you use nearby-search features and provide location permission, your device location may be used to determine relevant nearby businesses and display search results based on distance.

SearchMee does not require continuous background location tracking for ordinary nearby-shop search.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share or allow access to information with service providers that help us operate SearchMee, such as providers for:

  • Authentication and OTP services
  • Cloud hosting and server infrastructure
  • Analytics and diagnostics
  • Payment processing
  • Notifications
  • Technical and security services
  • Location-related services, where applicable

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services to SearchMee. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of SearchMee and its users.

5. Business Listings

Information submitted by shop/business owners for a public business listing may be displayed to SearchMee users.

  • Business name
  • Category
  • Business location/address
  • Business contact details
  • Business hours
  • Business photos
  • Other information intentionally provided for public display

Business owners should not submit confidential or unnecessary personal information as part of a public business listing.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account & Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their SearchMee account and associated personal information.

From the SearchMee app:
Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain for legitimate purposes such as legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial records.

Full steps are also available at searchmee.in/#delete-account.

8. User Choices

  • Update your account information
  • Control device location permissions
  • Disable certain notifications through device settings
  • Request account and data deletion
  • Contact us regarding your personal information

Disabling certain permissions may affect some SearchMee features.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

When we make changes, we may update the “Last Updated” date shown at the beginning of this policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at:

Delete Your SearchMee Account

App: SearchMee (Android) · Developer: SearchMee

Account deletion is available inside the SearchMee app. It is not available on the website, because your account is managed from the app.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the SearchMee app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to Account Management and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when asked. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account

What gets deleted

  • Your SearchMee account
  • Associated personal information linked to your account (such as your profile details and any information you submitted through the app)

We delete or anonymize applicable personal information after receiving a valid deletion request.

What may be kept

We may retain certain information only where we are legally required or permitted to, for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Legal compliance
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Dispute resolution
  • Financial and payment records

This information is kept only for as long as needed for these purposes and is not used for any other reason.

Can’t access the app?

If you can no longer open the app or log in, email us from your registered contact details at Searchmee001@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”, and include the mobile number linked to your account. We will verify the request and process it.

For details on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
